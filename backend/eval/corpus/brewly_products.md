# Cold Brew Starter Kit

The Brewly Cold Brew Starter Kit costs 29 dollars. It includes a 1 liter glass steeping jar, a
reusable stainless steel filter, and a 250 gram bag of coarse ground beans.

# Brewing Method

Brewly cold brew steeps coarse ground coffee in cold filtered water for 18 hours. The recommended
ratio is 1 part coffee to 8 parts water. Concentrate keeps for 10 days in the refrigerator.
