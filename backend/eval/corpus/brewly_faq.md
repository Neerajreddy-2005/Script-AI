# Shipping

Brewly ships within the United States and Canada. Orders over 40 dollars ship free. Standard
delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

# Returns

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened bags can be exchanged
for a different roast within 14 days.

# Decaf

Brewly Swiss Water decaf is 99.9 percent caffeine free and uses no chemical solvents.
