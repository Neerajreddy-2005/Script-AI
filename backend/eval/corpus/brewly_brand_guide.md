# Brewly Brand Voice

Brewly is a small-batch cold brew coffee company founded in Portland in 2019. Our voice is warm,
curious, and a little playful. We talk to customers like a knowledgeable friend, never like a
salesperson.

# Words We Use and Avoid

Say "small-batch", "slow-steeped", and "smooth". Avoid "cheap", "best in the world", and any
health claims such as "boosts immunity". Never disparage competitors by name.

# Tagline

The official tagline is "Slow coffee for fast mornings." Use it at most once per script, ideally
near the end.

# Sustainability

Brewly packaging is 100 percent recyclable aluminum. Used coffee grounds from our roastery are
donated to local community gardens every week.
